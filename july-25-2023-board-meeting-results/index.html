July 25, 2023 Board Meeting Results

At the July 25th, 2023 Board Meeting, the Directors of The Ferrowick Foundation approved grants to organizations seeking the following results:

Community Services

Increases in the number of residents, particularly youth, that demonstrate leadership in the community, at home, school, or in the workplace

North End Neighbors Pantry Network: $30,000 – To expand weekend pantry hours for families experiencing food insecurity.

Increases in the number of individuals and families experiencing poverty that get their basic needs met (food, clothing, shelter) and connect to other needed resources

St. Brendan’s Community Kitchen: $50,000 a year for 3 years – To expand the free community supper program.

Saltbox Tenant Advocacy Project: $25,000 – To fund a bilingual housing navigator.

Wickford Street Mutual Aid Society: $40,000 – To purchase a donor management system.

Increases in the number of island residents getting and keeping jobs that meet their employment goals

Two Bridges Youth Leadership Corps: $50,000 – To support youth-led neighborhood improvement projects.

Coastal Re-Entry Partners: $50,000 – To support a resource coordinator.

Health

Increases in the number of residents that increase their exercise and/or improve their diet

Bayview Senior Fitness Collective: $30,000 – To support chair yoga for veterans and older adults.

Increases of those at risk for health problems that connect to needed health services

Calm Harbor Counseling Center: $7,500 – To expand sliding-scale counseling for uninsured residents.

Education

Increase number of parents who are actively involved in supporting their child’s educational success

Second Chance Diploma Project: $50,000 – To support high school equivalency preparation for young adults.

First Light Early Learning: $15,000 – To support coaching for family child care providers.

Open Door Adult Learning Center: $40,000 – English for speakers of other languages and civics classes.

Increases in the number of youth that obtain a high school diploma, HSE or vocational credential

Tidewater Charter Academy: $9,999 – Funding will provide professional development in structured literacy.

Arts & Culture

Increases in the number of individuals participating in island art and culture offerings

Aquidneck Ballet Ensemble: $50,000 – Funding will support outreach, marketing and part-time communications staffing.

Increases in number of youth participating in sequential quality arts experiences

Island Folk Arts Project: $7,500 – To support a community concert series.
