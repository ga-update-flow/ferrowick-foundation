The Keystone Matching Grant Program Guidelines

We have been delighted by the response to our announcement of the Keystone Matching Grant Program. Our hope is that this incentive will encourage island grantees to take stock of the relationships and resources around them and try a fundraising approach that is new to them, strengthening their long-term sustainability. Many organizations have already begun applications, and we expect a competitive pool.

Below are the UPDATED program guidelines. The updates clarify the Foundation’s interest in new fundraising strategies and respond to questions we have heard from applicants. Working with the Board members who serve as the program’s Advisory Committee, we have added answers to:

  • Is there a limit on how much the Foundation will match for a single applicant?
  • What counts as “new money”?
  • When can an applicant begin raising funds that will qualify for a match?

Please read the guidelines carefully. They will guide your proposal and will be used by the Foundation to evaluate submissions. Please share them with everyone in your organization who is helping prepare your proposal.

Reminder: The application deadline is Friday, July 25.

Let us know if you would like to schedule a time to talk through your proposal ideas.

PROGRAM GUIDELINES

Updated June 9, 2025
Replaces all earlier versions

PROGRAM GOALS: Island nonprofits deliver essential services, yet few have the time, staff or flexible funding to invest in their own fundraising infrastructure. This year the Foundation is offering an incentive for island grantees to mobilize the people around them—board members, staff, volunteers, donors and participants—to try new ways of raising money, with Foundation dollars matching what they raise as unrestricted operating support. The program’s purpose is to help organizations build lasting fundraising capacity by trying something they have not tried before.

ELIGIBILITY TO APPLY: To be eligible, a nonprofit organization must:

  • be recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as a 501(c)(3) organization
  • be based on Aquidneck Island
  • have received a Ferrowick Foundation grant of $7,500 or more during the last four years and satisfactorily completed all grant requirements
  • have an active Board of Directors whose members will each make a personal financial contribution to the organization during the current fiscal year.

Applicants may hold a current Foundation grant. This program is an additional resource, not a substitute for regular grants.

WHAT MAY BE MATCHED: The Foundation will match funds raised from non-governmental sources that are new to the organization, or that represent an increase from an existing source when the increase results from a new strategy. Examples include:

  • Strategies that increase the number and size of employer matching gifts
  • New foundation or corporate grants, especially those secured through a board member’s or supporter’s relationship
  • Revenue from new membership campaigns, particularly those in which current members recruit new members
  • Giving campaigns, beyond regular membership dues, annual appeals or board giving, that take a new approach
  • Revenue from new crowdfunding, social media or website strategies

Other strategies will be considered as part of the fundraising plan in the application. A committee of Foundation Board members will work with staff to answer questions not covered by these guidelines. The members of the Keystone Matching Grant Program Advisory Committee are: Margaret Halloran Price, Desmond T. Okafor, Corinne B. Ashby, and Walter J. Pemberton, Jr.

WHAT DO WE MEAN BY “NEW MONEY?”: We are asking grantees to stretch by trying a strategy they have not used before. That can be an entirely new activity or a new twist on an existing one—for example, adding a monthly giving option to a year-end appeal, adding a live auction to an annual dinner, offering an incentive in a membership drive, or mailing to a neighborhood or community you have not asked before. Previous donors qualify for a match when their gift responds to a new strategy.

REQUEST LIMITATION: The Keystone Matching Grant Program will match new funds on a 2:1 basis for up to the first $200,000 raised. For example, if an approved organization raises $10,000, the Foundation will provide $20,000. If an organization is approved to raise $200,000 in new money, the Foundation’s match would total $400,000. Organizations may raise more, but the Foundation match is limited to the first $200,000 raised. We encourage applicants to set goals that are ambitious but realistic. The Foundation may approve less than the amount requested; the number of awards will depend on the number of applications, the amounts requested and the quality of the proposals.

PROGRAM TIMELINE:

  • April 2025 – Applications released
  • July 25, 2025 – Application deadline
  • October 7, 2025 – Awards announced
  • October 2025 – June 30, 2026 – Grantees submit documentation of matching funds

Applications are due July 25. Matching funds must be raised after the Foundation Board approves the application. The Foundation will accept documentation of matching funds through June 30, 2026.

PROGRAM SPECIFICS:

Applications will include an estimate of the amount the organization plans to raise for the Foundation to match.

Applications should include a detailed fundraising plan that describes:

  • a timeline
  • the strategy and the source of new funds
  • the people and partners involved
  • and the estimated cost of raising the funds

The Foundation will not match the value of in-kind donations.

To receive matching funds, grantees must submit evidence of new funds raised, such as:

  • copies of checks with a note from the donor
  • an award letter from a funder
  • ACH payment receipts that reference the match
  • or other documentation approved by the Foundation

Matching funds may be requested in a single payment or in monthly installments.

Keystone grantees will be required to submit a final report.

Funds raised may be used for general operating support or for any program the grantee chooses.

As always, please contact us with questions. Questions about the Keystone Matching Grant Program may be directed to Tobias Mendel-Ayers, Special Projects Manager, at tmendel-ayers@example.org.
